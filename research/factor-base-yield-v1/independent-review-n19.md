**Adversarial findings and required treatment.**

1. **F1 — Timing label: resolved by additive correction, wording still matters.** The 3.06× headline is supported only as a ratio of mixed-boundary totals. Use the solver-only ratio of medians 3.093× and separately report the two fresh pipeline comparisons. Say “ratio of medians,” not “median paired speedup.” Preserve the original artifacts and the correction together.
2. **F2 — Setup and oracle dependence: material limit on claim.** The exact subgroup oracle already contains all toy logs. The verified extraction remains a real relation/matrix computation, but no measured complete program or implementable cryptographic-scale attack cost follows. Before making an attack-level performance claim, run a public-input collector without the full-log oracle in its timed path, keep validation external, and record preprocessing, target generation, relation collection, linear algebra, memory and total wall time separately.
3. **F3 — Three streams are exploratory evidence.** Both fresh seeds start at zero rank and are frozen in the replication protocol; all 96 targets regenerate exactly from `Random(seed+19)`. Within an arm the processed target indices are an unbroken prefix; no reachable-target prefilter or evidence of cherry-picking within those prefixes was found. Uniform `a,b` produce a uniform subgroup target before conditioning on `R≠O`, even for the fixed known secret. Still, the original stream informed the decision to replicate, and all streams share one `P,Q`, one field, selected bases, and implementation. Two fresh streams and three wins do not establish a population speedup or scaling trend. A later confirmatory experiment should use more predeclared seeds and instances, repeated timing, fixed reporting rules and all outcomes.
4. **F4 — Valid paired control, incomplete attribution.** Both arms use the same archived Gaussian binary, `-x`, 30-second watchdog and maximum 96 targets, and stop at the same mathematical success criterion. Fresh active-arm order is shuffled with a separate seeded generator. The admission prefix was run arm-by-arm and plain-before-Gaussian, so its order is not equally balanced. The common binary SHA-256 is `64ef817176c5ebf0dc4623d20790b88efb8ced65920471cb5ad153ffada1adbc`. Gaussian was selected after plain-mode timeouts; both arms receive the same selected mode. Trace-k7 is an actual nondominated point of the recorded structural frontier, not a demonstrated globally optimal solver control. Dimension, density, basis directions, exact-clause counts and Frobenius schedule all change together. Attribute the comparison to the complete configurations; add fixed-dimension, fixed-schedule and solver-order ablations before attributing it specifically to density selection or claiming the best available method.
5. **F5 — Reuse is legitimate but not a third fresh execution.** Each arm's 12 reused rows exactly matches the Gaussian admission records in all original fields, including 11 relations and one UNSAT. They were reverified and incorporated once, and both new streams start with no reused rows. Keep the exploratory-plus-two-fresh description. The earlier 24 plain-mode timeouts remain operational evidence about those settings and were not charged to the selected Gaussian extraction totals; they establish no mathematical hardness or algorithmic lower bound.
6. **F6 — Archive provenance is useful but incomplete for performance replication.** All three runner, adapter and configuration hashes match their manifests; the same native solver binary is archived in all runs and the original admission. Source, profile constants and make rules are committed. The run manifests do not bind every imported source module or record a complete compiler/OS/CPU/thermal/concurrency environment and per-run whole-process timing. The snapshot preserves the current import implementations, but is not a compiler build attestation. Future benchmarks should archive a complete dependency/build manifest and environment alongside the inputs. No independent solver timing rerun is claimed in this review.

The chained model's finite auxiliary and sign recovery should also retain explicit exceptional-case treatment if generalized. Successful model checks and semantic UNSAT checks settle the recorded instances; they are not a general completeness proof of the encoder for every target or parameter set. In particular, a decomposition with an intermediate point at infinity cannot simply be represented by an ordinary finite auxiliary x coordinate. No observed result here depends on assuming such a theorem.

**Prior art and forbidden extrapolations.** SAT encoding, XOR/Gaussian reasoning and representation-specific branching already appear in Trimoska, Ionica and Dequen, [A SAT-Based Approach for Index Calculus on Binary Elliptic Curves](https://eprint.iacr.org/2019/313.pdf), especially Proposition 1 and the experimental section. That proposition concerns their symmetric model; it cannot be imported as a theorem that the present S3-chain auxiliary variables need no search. The source correctly searches them. Paper timing comparisons are not reproduced by these artifacts.

Affine factor spaces and efforts to increase relation yield already appear in Galbraith and Gebregiyorgis, [Summation polynomial algorithms for elliptic curves in characteristic two](https://eprint.iacr.org/2014/806.pdf), Section 6, Experiment 3. That work uses a different binary Edwards representation; it is relevant prior art for the mechanism class, not proof that this exact density-selected trace-affine basis or schedule was previously published.

Factor-base dimension tuning and even different factor spaces within one decomposition are explicit in Diem, [On the discrete logarithm problem in elliptic curves II](https://msp.org/ant/2013/7-6/ant-v7-n6-p01-s.pdf), pages 1286–1287. Frobenius-aware factor bases and rewriting relations into orbit representatives are developed by Galbraith, Granger, Merz and Petit, [On index calculus algorithms for subfield curves](https://sacworkshop.org/SAC20/files/preproceedings/18-IndexCalculus.pdf), Sections 2–4. The present image-space schedule is not identical to constructing an invariant base and quotienting all its orbits, but the automorphism and coefficient-rewrite ingredients have substantial prior art. This review does not establish novelty of their combination.

No asymptotic ECDLP improvement, cryptographic-parameter break, advantage over automorphism-aware Pollard rho, general superiority of Gaussian WDSat, or causal benefit from density alone is established. These measurements cannot support a first-fall-degree or solving-degree scaling conclusion. SAT/UNSAT success, a timeout of another solver, and recovery of a known toy scalar do not bridge those missing arguments.
